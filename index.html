CONTRACT FOR INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY HARDWARE

Agreement No. CTR-000306  |  Icertis LA-000306  |  Date: October 10, 2024

Parties: SAP (Buyer) and Tatra Corporation (Supplier)

1. Definitions & Interpretation

"Agreement" means this Contract for Information Technology Hardware including all schedules. "Goods" means the items listed in Schedule A. "Delivery Date" means the date specified for delivery of Goods.

2. Payment Terms

Payment shall be made within Net 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. All payments shall be in USD. Late payment shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. Delivery & Acceptance

3.1 Delivery Schedule
Supplier shall deliver all items within 60 days of contract execution to Buyer-specified locations.

3.2 Acceptance Criteria
Buyer has 30 days to inspect and accept or reject goods. Rejection shall be in writing.

5. Confidentiality
